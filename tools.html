Plan your night

Know what’s up, and when it’s worth heading out.

Dark Sky Planner screenshot coming soon

Dark Sky Planner

See exactly when it gets properly dark tonight, when the Moon rises and sets, and how much Moon-free darkness you’ll get. It also picks out the best dark nights of the coming month.

Planets Tonight screenshot coming soon

Planets Tonight

When each planet rises, when it’s highest and how well placed it is tonight, with a simple rating. A day-by-day calendar helps you plan weeks ahead.

What’s Up Tonight screenshot coming soon

What’s Up Tonight

The best of the 110 famous Messier galaxies, nebulae and star clusters to see or image tonight from where you are, filtered to suit your telescope. Tick them off in your own Messier log.

Moon Phase screenshot coming soon

Moon Phase

The Moon’s phase right now, or on any date and time you choose.

Meteor Showers screenshot coming soon

Meteor Showers

When each shower peaks, how many meteors to expect, whether moonlight will spoil it, and the best time to look from your location.

Planets up close

For the nights you’re chasing detail on Jupiter, Saturn and Mars.

Jupiter’s Moons screenshot coming soon

Jupiter’s Moons

Watch Io, Europa, Ganymede and Callisto move around Jupiter, and get alerts when a moon or its shadow crosses the face of the planet, including rare nights when two shadows cross at once.

Great Red Spot screenshot coming soon

Great Red Spot

Find out when Jupiter’s Great Red Spot faces Earth, so you can catch it at its best.

Planetary Imaging screenshot coming soon

Planetary Imaging

See each planet’s size, which side of Jupiter or Mars is facing you, and the tilt of Saturn’s rings. It also checks whether your camera and Barlow give the right magnification, and how long you can record before the planet’s spin blurs the detail.

Your equipment

Using the kit already saved in your Equipment Manager.

Eyepiece Calculator screenshot coming soon

Eyepiece Calculator

Pick a telescope and eyepiece from your saved equipment to see the magnification, how much sky you’ll see and what each combination is best for, with the whole Moon drawn to scale in the view.

Astrophotography Calculator screenshot coming soon

Astrophotography Calculator

Check that your camera and telescope are a good match, see your field of view, and find the longest exposure before stars start to trail. It covers planetary, deep-sky and camera-and-lens photography, including the limits for alt-az GoTo mounts.

Polar Alignment screenshot coming soon

Polar Alignment

Shows exactly where to place Polaris in your mount’s polar scope right now, with a simple guide if you’re new to equatorial mounts.

FAQ

Questions about the tools.

Is AstroSphere free?

Yes. The social app is free to download and use. The subscription only unlocks the Tools section.

What do I get with the subscription?

All eleven tools on this page, in one place, for one monthly price.

Do I need internet at my observing site?

Most tools do their calculations on your phone, so they work offline at a dark site once the app is installed. Moon Phase needs a connection.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. You can cancel at any time in your App Store subscription settings.

Is it only for experts?

No. Every tool explains its results in plain language. If you’re just starting out, open Planets Tonight or What’s Up Tonight and you’ll know what to look at tonight.
