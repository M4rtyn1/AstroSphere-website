Accuracy and safety

  • The tools work out positions, times, forecasts and equipment figures from published models and data. We check them carefully, but they’re estimates, and weather forecasts can be wrong. Don’t rely on them where safety or money is at stake.

  • Observe responsibly: respect private land and access rules, and take care when observing alone, at night or in remote places.
