AstroSphere is a new, free social app for space lovers. We’re inviting 500 founding members to shape it from the start: to post, explore, tell us what works and what doesn’t, and make it feel like a real community from day one.

What we ask

Nothing heavy. Just be part of it.

Experts and complete beginners are equally welcome. What matters is that you’ll actually use it.

  • Use AstroSphere and try the tools
  • Share your photos, observations and questions
  • Comment on and encourage other members
  • Tell us about bugs
  • Give honest feedback, good and bad
Astronomy clubs and societies

Bring your club with you.

AstroSphere is built to support clubs, not replace them. Your club can have its own Squad to share session reports and keep talking between meetings. If your club would like to join as a group, mention it in your email and we’ll talk about becoming a Founding Club.

How to apply

One email. That’s it.

  1. Email us at support@astro-sphere.co.uk with the subject “Founding 500”.
  2. Tell us a little about you: your name, where you’re based, the kind of astronomy you enjoy and any club you belong to.
  3. We’ll reply with what happens next. Places are given in the order applications arrive.
Apply by emailsupport@astro-sphere.co.uk

Founding membership is limited to 500 people and closes for good once full. You must be 13 or over to use AstroSphere. Lifetime access covers the AstroSphere Tools subscription; any separate products we might offer in the future aren’t included.
