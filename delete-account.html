Delete your AstroSphere account

AstroSphere, by Martyn Clarkson · Last updated 31 October 2026

Option 1: in the app (quickest)

  • Open AstroSphere and sign in.
  • Tap the gear icon on SkyFeed, then Settings → Delete account. (Or go to Edit Profile → Delete account.)
  • You may be asked to sign in again to confirm it’s you. Then confirm the deletion.

Option 2: by email (if you can’t use the app)

Email support@astro-sphere.co.uk from the email address linked to your account, with the subject “Delete my AstroSphere account”. Include your @username if you know it. We’ll confirm and delete your account within 30 days, usually much sooner.

What gets deleted

  • Your sign-in account, profile, @username and date of birth
  • Your posts and reposts, with their comments, photos and notifications
  • Your comments (a comment with replies becomes “This comment was deleted”, with no author), likes, follows, crewmates and requests
  • Notifications to or from you, all your photos, gear, setups, Messier log and blocks
  • Your push tokens, launch reminders and your record at our subscription service (RevenueCat)

Deleted data is gone from our database within about an hour. We don’t keep backups.

What we keep

  • Reports filed by or about you, for up to 12 months after they’re resolved, for safety.
  • Catalogue suggestions you sent, in anonymised form.
  • Crash reports (not linked to your account) are deleted automatically after 90 days.

Only want to delete some data?

You can delete individual posts, comments and photos in the app at any time, or email us to ask for specific data to be deleted without closing your account.

Subscriptions

Deleting your account doesn’t cancel an App Store or Google Play subscription. Cancel it in your store account settings first.

More detail is in our Privacy Policy.
