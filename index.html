Why AstroSphere

A smaller, better social network. That’s the point.

The big platforms were built to hold anybody’s attention for as long as possible. Astronomy is slow, patient and quiet, and it was never going to win that fight. So we built somewhere it doesn’t have to.

Wall-to-wall night sky

No crossover content, no adverts between the nebulae, no politics, no influencers.

You choose the feed

Posts arrive newest first. Tap a category and the feed becomes only that. No mystery ranking.

Gear on the picture

Every post can carry the telescope and camera that took it, so “what did you shoot that with?” is already answered.

Properly threaded talk

Comments nest into replies, so a back-and-forth about guiding errors stays readable.

Small rooms

Your peace, your rules

The Sky Feed

Filter the whole universe down to tonight’s mood.

Everything the community captured last night lands in the Sky Feed. Across the top sit the category filters. One tap and the feed narrows. Want nothing but galaxies for twenty minutes? You can have exactly that.

  • Post in seconds. Tap +Post, add one photo or a whole session’s worth, and pick a category.
  • Edit or delete any time. No typo lives forever.
  • Like, comment and reply. Helpful answers rise to the top for the next person.

Squads

Astronomy has always been a club hobby.

A Squad is a group with its own name, picture, description and private feed. Your local society, a Dobsonian owners’ group, the narrowband crowd, the people chasing every lunar eclipse.

  • Start one in a minute. Name it, describe it, pick a photo. It’s yours to shape.
  • Join or request. Private Squads send your request to the owner, and you’re notified when they accept.
  • Post inside it. Session reports, questions, dark-sky trip plans. What’s said in the Squad stays in the Squad.
  • Run it well. Owners appoint moderators, approve requests and remove anyone who doesn’t belong.
Crewmates & followers

Crewmates are your inner circle: a mutual connection you both agreed to. Following is lighter: see someone’s posts with no request and no obligation.

Daily wonder & calm notifications

NASA’s picture of the day, and notifications you control.

Every day NASA publishes an extraordinary image of the cosmos, explained by professional astronomers. AstroSphere puts it at the top of your feed with its title, story and credit.

Then AstroSphere tells you only what involves you: likes, comments and replies, new followers, crewmate requests and Squad decisions. Every type can be switched off on its own.

How do I delete my account?

Open My Page, choose Edit Profile, then Delete account. Your profile, posts, comments and equipment list are removed. If you can’t sign in, email us from the address on your account.

How do I make my account private?

Switch your account to private in your profile settings. Only people you approve can then see what you share. You can switch back at any time.

How do I report a post or user?

Email us with the username and a description of the post. We review every report.

I forgot my password

On the Sign In screen tap “Forgot password?” and we’ll email you a reset link.
